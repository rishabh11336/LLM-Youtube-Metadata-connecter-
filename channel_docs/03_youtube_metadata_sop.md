# 📹 YouTube Metadata Creation SOP

> **This is the locked instruction set for generating Titles, Descriptions, and Tags for all Punji the Labrador videos. Follow verbatim.**

---

## Purpose

This SOP defines how Titles, Descriptions, and Tags must be written for all videos on the channel, ensuring:
- ✅ SEO correctness
- ✅ Long-term algorithm trust
- ✅ Human authenticity
- ✅ Zero AI-content misrepresentation
- ✅ Compatibility with both public and unlisted videos
- ✅ Alignment with the algorithmic-philanthropy persona

**This SOP is channel-level, not video-level.**

---

## Hard Constraints (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### Content Authenticity
- All videos depict **real events, real dogs, real people**
- ❌ No AI-generated visuals, scripts, reenactments, or simulations
- ❌ No misleading phrasing implying rescue, danger, or drama if it did not happen
- Language must reflect **what actually occurred**

### Description Length
- **Maximum**: 5,000 characters (YouTube's limit)
- **Target range**: 1,200–3,500 characters unless context requires more

### Language Quality
- Human, natural tone
- No spelling errors
- No grammatical inconsistencies
- No robotic or template-like phrasing
- **English first**, Hinglish only where culturally natural

---

## Inputs Required (Per Video)

1. **Pre-Title** — Raw or working title (may be unoptimized; used as semantic intent reference)
2. **YouTube Video Link** — Can be public or unlisted (treated identically for metadata quality)

No other inputs required.

---

## Outputs Generated (Every Time)

Exactly **three assets** per video:

```
TITLE: ...

DESCRIPTION: ...

TAGS: ...
```

---

## Title Creation Rules (SEO + CTR)

### Technical Rules
- Length: **60–70 characters**
- Primary keyword in **first 40 characters**
- Readable on mobile
- No keyword stuffing
- No ALL CAPS

### Semantic Rules
- Must reflect **real action shown**
- Emotional but not exaggerated
- No false urgency or rescue framing

### Structural Formula
```
[Real Action / Emotion] + [Primary Keyword] | [Context]
```

### Titles Must AVOID
- ❌ "You won't believe…"
- ❌ "Shocking…"
- ❌ "This will make you cry"
- ❌ Any implication of danger, abuse, or rescue unless explicitly shown

---

## Description Creation Rules (MOST IMPORTANT)

Descriptions must balance **human storytelling + SEO indexing + ethical clarity**.

### Mandatory 5-Section Structure

#### 🔹 Section 1: First 2 Lines (Search & Suggested Engine)
- Clear, simple, human sentence
- Primary keywords placed naturally
- **Must stand alone in previews** — these lines determine discoverability

#### 🔹 Section 2: Real-World Context (Humanized Narrative)
- Explain what is happening in the video
- Mention: Feeding, Interaction, Care, Routine
- Written like a real person explaining a real moment
- ❌ No cinematic language
- ❌ No fabricated emotional arcs

#### 🔹 Section 3: Channel & Welfare Context
- Light explanation of: Punji, Neighbourhood dogs, Daily care
- Emphasis on routine, responsibility, and continuity

#### 🔹 Section 4: Ethical Viewer Engagement
- Optional soft CTA
- Examples:
  - "Watching and understanding matters"
  - "Small actions help street dogs live better lives"
- ❌ No donation pressure
- ❌ No guilt language

#### 🔹 Section 5: SEO Footer (Readable, Not Spam)
- Natural keyword clusters
- Channel identity reinforcement
- Consistent phrasing across videos

### Description Quality Checklist (Enforced)
- ✅ ≤5000 characters
- ✅ Spell-checked
- ✅ Grammar-checked
- ✅ Human tone
- ✅ SEO-clean
- ✅ Safe for unlisted videos becoming public later

---

## Tag Creation Rules

### Tag Categories
- Exact-match keywords
- Broad discovery terms
- Long-tail intent phrases
- Channel authority tags

### Language Mix
- English (primary)
- Hinglish (contextual)

### Tag Restrictions
- ❌ No trending but irrelevant tags
- ❌ No misleading rescue or abuse tags
- ❌ No repetition spam

---

## Channel-Level Context (Always Assumed)

All metadata must align with these channel truths:
- Daily morning feeding
- On-demand feeding at home
- Street dog trust & habituation
- Geriatric female dog medical care (tubectomy)
- Real family-funded welfare model
- **Joy-based, non-performative compassion**

> This context does not need to be restated fully in every video, but it must **never be contradicted**.

---

## Failure Conditions (When Output is Invalid)

Output must be **rewritten** if:
- ❌ Description exceeds 5,000 characters
- ❌ Title implies something not shown
- ❌ Language feels AI-generated or templated
- ❌ Spelling/grammar errors exist
- ❌ Metadata misaligns with channel ethics

---

## Execution Rule

> Every title, description, and tag must **help the algorithm understand the video without lying**, **help the viewer without manipulation**, and **help real dogs without spectacle**.
